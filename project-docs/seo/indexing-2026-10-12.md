## Research and fixes 2026-10-21 (afternoon)

Correction to the readout above: the Pages report's last update is 2026-10-11, so "26 indexed" is stale,
not a stall. Live URL Inspection on 12 pages: 7 indexed (mahj/, posture/, the vitals, mahj, headaches and
cribbage guides, the bridge comparison). Not indexed: sober/ (crawled), quitzyn guide (crawled), mahj and
headaches comparisons (crawled), conduit-fill guide (discovered). Rejected pages are not near-duplicates
(under 15% shingle overlap between comparison pages); most are health topics on a new domain.

Research summary (evidence strength in brackets):
1. Google AI features need nothing beyond normal indexing; llms.txt and FAQ schema are neutral [strong].
   developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
2. "Crawled - not indexed" is a quality or duplicate verdict (Gary Illyes, SEJ May 2024); Request Indexing
   does not change it [moderate to strong].
3. Internal links help discovery [moderate].
4. Third-party mentions (Reddit, YouTube) correlate most with AI visibility (Ahrefs, 75k brands)
   [moderate, correlational].
5. Answer in the first sentences: 44% of ChatGPT citations come from the first 30% of a page
   (Growth Memo, 18k citations) [moderate]. Question headings alone have only vendor data [weak to moderate].
6. Statistics, quotes and cited sources raised visibility 28-43% in the GEO paper (arXiv 2311.09735) [moderate].
7. ChatGPT search uses OAI-SearchBot (robots.txt already allows it); the Bing link is disputed.
