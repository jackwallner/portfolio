# Sports Cheat Sheet changelog

> Every Sports Cheat Sheet update, newest first.

## Version 1.1.7 (November 18, 2026)

Now on iPad, with a layout designed for the larger screen.

## Version 1.1.6 (November 9, 2026)

Briefings stay fresh and honest. If a refresh can't reach the server, you now see the saved briefing with its age instead of an offline error, and free users keep their daily refresh. Pro shows the real time of your next refresh. Smoother motion with Reduce Motion on, bigger tap targets, clearer contrast, and better layouts at large text sizes.

## Version 1.1.5 (November 3, 2026)

Briefings refresh when you come back to the app, show how fresh they are, and load reliably on iOS 17 and 18. Text is easier to read and buttons are easier to tap throughout.

## Version 1.1.4 (October 30, 2026)

Behind-the-scenes improvements and bug fixes for a smoother experience. Thank you for being here.

## Version 1.1.3 (September 8, 2026)

Gist now lets you read your first free briefing before showing Pro. We also simplified onboarding, clarified the subscription choices, and refreshed the App Store presentation around what the app actually gives you: what happened, why it matters, and one thing to say.
