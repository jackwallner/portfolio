Privacy Policy

Updated November 20, 2026.

Advent Doors does not collect, store or share personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Your calendar, the doors you open and your ratings stay on your device. On the web they live in your browser's local storage; in the iPhone app they live in the app's storage on your phone.

When you make a calendar for someone, the names, note and chosen days are written into the link itself. Anyone you send the link to can see them. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

If you turn on reminders in the iPhone app, they are scheduled on your phone by iOS.
