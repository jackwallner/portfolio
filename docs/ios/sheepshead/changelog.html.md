> Every Sheepshead Trainer update, newest first.

## Version 1.3.3 (November 9, 2026)

Undo on flashcards now also removes the grade for that answer, so an undone tap no longer counts as a miss or counts twice as correct. The game night reminder now stops after your membership lapses and is kept when it renews.

## Version 1.3.0 (October 23, 2026)

Sharper practice, a clearer week.

Who Takes the Trick? deals five-card tricks in Endless Practice and asks which card wins, with the rule behind every answer. A second new skill drills counting the points.

Each room shows how much you have truly locked in, and Home counts the questions you locked in this week.

Also new: Send Feedback in Settings, Reduce Motion support and clearer membership terms.

## Version 1.2.2 (August 24, 2026)

A smarter rhythm for card night.

Sheepshead Minute is a new daily challenge. Every member gets the same five questions on the same day: two hand reads, one bury decision, and two trick calls. Share your score, and keep a forgiving five-day weekly rhythm with a 30-day archive to catch up from.

Game Night Prep lets you set your usual card night. A reminder arrives beforehand and opens straight into a five-minute session built from your due mistakes, your weakest room, and material you have not seen yet.

Sheepshead Trainer now runs natively on iPad, in portrait or landscape.

Sheepshead Minute and Game Night Prep are part of Sheepshead+.
