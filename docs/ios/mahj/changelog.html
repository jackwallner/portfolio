Mahj Trainer changelog

Version 1.4.4

November 16, 2026

The Monthly Challenge now shows on Home from your first visit.

The Training tiles are cleaner and easier to read.

Version 1.4.2

October 28, 2026

A drill you leave now waits for you, on the same question.

On the unfolded iPhone Duo, Home and your drill sit side by side.

Every answer fits on smaller iPhones, and the largest text sizes read cleanly.

The Mahj Minute rolls over at midnight and on the first of the month without losing a point.

Two answers were corrected: the junk pass in the Charleston and the dead quint with jokers.

Reduce Motion is respected everywhere.

Version 1.4.1

October 27, 2026

Every practice and challenge question has been rechecked, the explanations are clearer, and the monthly challenge has fresh questions lined up through next September.

Mahj Minute is now for everyone, and it counts.

Five new questions a day, the same for every player. Each day adds to the month's challenge: your best 20 days make your score, and a strong month shows where you rank. It appears on Home after your first practice.

Home and Your Progress now show how many questions you locked in this week, so you can see the practice working instead of guessing.

Members can set their game night straight from Home, and a prep reminder brings you back before you play.

Send Feedback is a text box and one button now. No mail app, no account.

Thank you for the notes and the ratings. They are how a one-person app gets found.

Version 1.3.2

October 13, 2026

Small fixes and behind-the-scenes improvements.

Version 1.3.1

October 6, 2026

Play a hand, one tile at a time.

Play a Hand deals you thirteen tiles off a real wall. Commit to a section, then draw and discard for twelve turns while a coach grades every throw and shows the arithmetic behind it. No opponents and no bots. Everyone gets one free hand a day.

Reference is a new searchable glossary plus a page for every section of the card, free for everyone, one tap from the home screen.

Room progress now counts what you have actually learned: a question sticks once you answer it right twice in a row.

Get one wrong and the app now tells you why your answer was wrong, not just what the right one was.

New in the Table Room: scoring and table rules. Who pays what, when a call is too late, and what happens after a wrong mah jongg.

New in the Card Room: how to read a line on the card, from matching and opposite dragons to NEWS and zeros.

We also re-checked every question against the rules and corrected a handful of answers, including a joker question and a defense question players flagged. Thank you for the notes.

Version 1.2.1

August 27, 2026

A smarter rhythm for game night.

Mahj Minute is a new daily challenge. Every member gets the same five questions on the same day: two rack reads, one Charleston call, and two table judgments. Share your score, and keep a forgiving five-day weekly rhythm with a 30-day archive to catch up from.

Game Night Prep lets you set your usual game night. A reminder arrives beforehand and opens straight into a five-minute session built from your due mistakes, your weakest room, and material you have not seen yet.

Mahj Trainer now runs natively on iPad, in portrait or landscape.

Mahj Minute and Game Night Prep are part of Mahj+.

Version 1.1

August 7, 2026

Practice that never runs out.

Endless Practice deals a fresh rack every time, so you can drill section reading and tile counting for as long as you like, with a full explanation after every answer.

Fix My Mistakes now remembers what you got wrong and brings it back on a schedule, so weak spots actually close.

Timed Challenge gives you ninety seconds and a personal best to chase.

Also new: an accuracy breakdown for every room, and more hand-written questions across the tiles, the card, the Charleston, the table, and the Master Tables.
