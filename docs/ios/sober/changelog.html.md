## Version 1.3.1 (September 6, 2026)

• Craving mode: ride out an urge with a few minutes of guided breathing. Free, no paywall.
• A slip no longer erases your garden. Your tree keeps its growth, your longest streak stands, and every sober day you've counted still counts.
• Logged a slip by mistake? You can take it back now, your counter, streak and tree all go back.
• Your patterns (Bloom+): when urges hit, what sets them off, and how long yours actually last.
• Money and calories kept so far are now free for everyone.
• Fixes: back-dated slips no longer change your longest streak, and the widget and Apple Watch now show the same tree as the app.
