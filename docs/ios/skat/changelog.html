Skat Trainer changelog

Version 1.3.2

November 8, 2026

Undo in the deck now takes back your answer, so accuracy and review schedules stay right. Reminder times and your streak stay put when you change time zones. Weekly gain no longer recounts Skat+ items after a lapse.

Version 1.3.1

October 31, 2026

More accurate trainer advice, shuffled answers in the Bedienpflicht drills, a fixed mistake review queue, German purchase messages, and better VoiceOver and small-screen layouts.

Version 1.3.0

October 22, 2026

Practice until it sticks.

Three new Endless Practice skills: counting card points, winning tricks in suit, Grand and Null games, and matadors (Spitzen). Every hand is freshly dealt.

After a wrong answer, a note explains exactly why your choice does not fit. Each room shows how much you have truly mastered, and Home counts the questions you locked in this week.

Also new: Send Feedback in Settings and clearer membership terms. Exercises are in German.

Version 1.2.3

September 30, 2026

More depth at the card table.

222 authored learning items in total. The Master Table is rebuilt with 100 concrete exercises covering game values, overbidding, defense, Null and endgames instead of basic recall questions.

24 new free cases cover bidding and trick rules. Every discard exercise now states a clear learning objective, with explanations that distinguish teaching goals from universally optimal play.

Daily generated hands are now consistent for the same date. Exercise content is in German.

Version 1.2.2

August 20, 2026

A better rhythm for Skat night.

The Skat Minute is the new daily round. Every member gets the same five questions on the same day: two hand reads, one discard decision, and two trick-play questions. Share your score, and keep a forgiving five-day weekly rhythm with a 30-day archive to catch up from.

Game Night Prep lets you set your usual Skat night. A reminder arrives beforehand and opens straight into a five-minute session built from your due mistakes, your weakest room, and material you have not seen yet.

Skat Trainer now runs natively on iPad, in portrait or landscape.

The Skat Minute and Game Night Prep are part of Skat+.
