# Baby Tracker changelog

> Every Baby Tracker update, newest first.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/baby/changelog.html

## Version 1.4.6 (November 24, 2026)

Bug fixes and improvements.

## Version 1.4.5 (November 16, 2026)

New: a Pump button, and buttons you name yourself. Add them in Settings.

## Version 1.4.4 (November 14, 2026)

Now on iPad. Fixes a crash that could stop the app from opening.

## Version 1.4.3 (November 9, 2026)

Easier-to-tap buttons, clearer text, and fixes for VoiceOver and translated screens.

## Version 1.4.2 (November 6, 2026)

Baby Tracker is now available in German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese.

## Version 1.4.1 (November 4, 2026)

Fixes a crash on Apple Watch, a crash when joining a shared log from an invite link, and a crash when tapping a reminder.

## Version 1.4 (October 23, 2026)

New: the All button logs a feed, a pee and a poop in one tap, and Settings > Feed types lets you choose breast, bottle or both. Feed reminders: pick a gap from 2 to 4 hours and get a nudge with the side to start on. History now shows Today and Yesterday at the top, one tap from that day's log, and Baby+ adds a chart you can drag through to see any day. Widget taps now reach your partner's phone right away. Plus a short optional setup question, and a note the evening before a checkup that your doctor summary is ready.

## Version 1.3 (October 11, 2026)

New Apple Watch app with complications: log a feed, diaper or sleep from your watch face in one tap, with Undo on the wrist. A compact bottom bar for Log, History, Reports and Settings. Preview the pediatrician report before you share it, and reports now leave out partial days.

## Version 1.2 (October 1, 2026)

More reliable widget logging, clearer tap confirmations, and improved Undo.

## Version 1.1 (September 29, 2026)

New: Feed is one tap, and the side is optional. Logging late? Wind the time back above the buttons, or add older entries from the bottom of the main screen. New Feed, Pee and Poop widgets log with one tap from the Home Screen or Lock Screen, with Undo right on the widget. Totals can start at the hour your day does, or cover the last 24 hours.

## Version 1.0 (September 25, 2026)

First release on the App Store.
