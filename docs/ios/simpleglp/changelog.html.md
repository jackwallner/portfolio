## Version 1.2.1 (October 1, 2026)

• Log without opening the app: tap “Took it” on a reminder, or tell Siri “Log my dose in Simple GLP”.
• Took it earlier? Log the real time and your pill wait counts from then. Tap a day in the week strip or the new History calendar to fill in a missed log.
• New Lock Screen widget: your next shot, today’s pill, or when your wait ends.
• Fixes for edited dose times, shots taken early, and the details sheet.

## Version 1.2.0 (September 26, 2026)

New: daily GLP-1 pills. Pick Wegovy pill, Rybelsus, Foundayo, or your own, and one tap logs today's pill and starts your wait timer on Home, the widget, and Apple Watch.
• Daily reminders skip days you've already logged, plus a streak and week view.
• Pro adds a Lock Screen and Dynamic Island countdown and supply tracking with refill reminders.
• Editing your plan now keeps your saved dose and wait time.

## Version 1.1.1 (September 21, 2026)

• History now lists months in date order.
• You'll see a message if a shot can't be deleted, instead of it quietly coming back.
• Your shot history is kept safe if the app's data file is ever damaged.
