# Pro Football changelog

> Every Pro Football update, newest first.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/football/changelog.html

## Version 1.2.10 (November 24, 2026)

Behind-the-scenes improvements and fixes to keep Pro Football running smoothly. Thank you for being here.

## Version 1.2.9 (November 8, 2026)

You can now change the app's language from Settings. This update also includes fixes and improvements.

## Version 1.2.8 (October 31, 2026)

Player search on the leaderboards now finds players at every position, not only the tab you have open. Purchase errors are clearer, and Terms and Privacy links sit next to every unlock.

## Version 1.2.7 (October 22, 2026)

Share any player's percentile board as an image, right from their profile.
Leaderboards, search and season switching are much faster, especially for past seasons.
Clearer pricing on the upgrade screen.

## Version 1.2.6 (October 14, 2026)

Bug fixes and improvements.

## Version 1.2.5 (October 13, 2026)

Game previews before kickoff: the projected line, win chance, a tale of the tape and each team's season leaders.
Team pages rank every club against the other 31, with totals per game.
Recent form and Trends compare per game, so byes and Thursday games no longer decide who is hot.
Defenders show pass-rush and coverage percentiles, plus many fixes across the app.

## Version 1.2.4 (October 9, 2026)

Behind-the-scenes improvements and fixes to keep Football Next running smoothly. Thank you for being here.

## Version 1.2.3 (September 29, 2026)

Position boards now open on each position’s default stat. Only a stat you choose carries over when you switch positions.

## Version 1.2.2 (September 28, 2026)

• Rankings show the playing-time minimum, volume, and when zero-count stats are unranked.
• Player pages add bio details, injury status, contract value, and a game log.
• Games and Teams show records, standings, power ratings, and projected margins.
• Trends starts with a three-week view and explains when movement begins.

## Version 1.2.1 (September 15, 2026)

Fixes for game day.
• Coming back after a few days now shows your latest saved stats.
• Game pages show a Try Again button when the advanced breakdown doesn't load.
• Stat rows on player pages opened from Compare now open their leaderboards.

## Version 1.2 (September 14, 2026)

Built for game day.
• New Games tab: scores, schedules and box scores for every game, with advanced EPA breakdowns.
• Stats opens on familiar leaders like passing yards, with advanced rankings one tap away.
• New Following view keeps your team and favorite players in one place, free.
• Search the whole leaderboard without losing a player's league rank.
• Fresh stats soon after games are published, and every screen shows the week and games included.

## Version 1.1.2 (September 11, 2026)

The 2026 season is live: every player who has played is ranked from week one and updated nightly. Choose View > Qualified to see only players with enough playing time.

## Version 1.1.1 (September 7, 2026)

Updated App Store information and subscription diagnostics.

## Version 1.1.0 (August 19, 2026)

Playoffs on every team page. The season type switch now follows you into a club's page, so a roster's postseason numbers read the same way its regular season does.

Recent form fixed. Last 3 / 5 / 8 game windows work on a finished season instead of coming back empty, and they no longer mix regular season and playoff games into one window.

A stat glossary you can find. Every player and team page now links to definitions for every stat in the app, and the glossary's own search works.

Also: the Compare page's season pickers no longer cut off the year, and the intro screens no longer truncate.

## Version 1.0 (August 16, 2026)

First release on the App Store.
