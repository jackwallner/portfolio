# Sunset & Sunrise Prediction changelog

> Every Sunset & Sunrise Prediction update, newest first.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/sunset/changelog.html

## Version 1.0.5 (November 17, 2026)

Now on iPad, with a layout designed for the larger screen.

## Version 1.0.4 (November 8, 2026)

Sunset alerts now stay out of your quiet hours, even far north. Alerts follow your iOS notification setting and the newest location. The paywall closes once Sun+ is restored, and onboarding no longer double-advances on quick taps.

## Version 1.0.3 (November 4, 2026)

More alerts for colorful sunsets and sunrises: the score now spots more skies with good color. Fixes and improvements.

## Version 1.0.2 (November 1, 2026)

Forecasts now stay correct across daylight saving time changes, so scores and alert times are right after the clocks change, and the days before spring forward have a forecast again. Low cloud toward the sun is now read for the right hour.

## Version 1.0.1 (October 27, 2026)

Behind-the-scenes improvements and bug fixes for a smoother experience. Thank you for being here.

## Version 1.0 (October 14, 2026)

First release on the App Store.
