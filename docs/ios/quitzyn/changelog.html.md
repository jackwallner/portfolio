## Version 1.3.5 (November 20, 2026)

New: the Nicotine-Free January challenge. From December 18 it sits at the top of Timeline. Every nicotine-free day of January fills in the month, and all 31 earn the January 2027 badge.

Also in this update: the day count on Home no longer shows its number twice, plus fixes and improvements.

## Version 1.2.5 (September 21, 2026)

• Adding a past slip no longer changes your current streak or your tree.
• Undo a mistaken slip right from the slip screen or Home.
• Ask to Buy purchases unlock once approved, and Restore tells you if nothing was found.
• Opening craving mode by accident no longer logs a craving.
• The app now tells you if something couldn't be saved.

## Version 1.2.4 (September 14, 2026)

New: craving mode. When an urge hits, start a short guided breathing session and ride it out. It's free.
A slip no longer wipes out your garden. Your counter restarts, your tree keeps half its growth, and a mistaken slip can be undone.
Bloom+ now shows your craving patterns.
Widgets and Apple Watch now match your tree, plus other fixes.
